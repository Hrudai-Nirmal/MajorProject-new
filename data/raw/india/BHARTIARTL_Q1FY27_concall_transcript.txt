--- FINANCIAL RESULTS ---
For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, Bharti Airtel reported consolidated net profit of Rs 8,167 crore, up 37% YoY, on consolidated revenue of Rs 58,539 crore, up 18.4% YoY (some sources cite a slightly higher PAT of Rs 8,424 crore, +42% YoY, in capital-markets coverage of the same quarter). EBITDA grew 19% YoY with margin widening to 57.4%. Average revenue per user (ARPU) rose to Rs 264 from Rs 250 a year earlier. The company added 14.9 million customers in the quarter, taking its global subscriber base to 681 million across 15 countries. Home broadband customers grew 34% YoY.

--- GUIDANCE / RISK SIGNAL ---
The quarter's sequential net-profit comparison "comes with a catch" per trade press — flagged as not a clean like-for-like base due to one-off items in prior quarters, a nuance analysts should account for. Sales and marketing spend rose 25% YoY to support customer acquisition, a cost pressure to watch against margin trajectory. Management's tariff approach was described as "restructuring unlimited-data plans rather than a blanket increase," signalling constrained pricing power / consumer sensitivity rather than aggressive ARPU-led hikes.

--- MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY ---
Management (attributed to Vice Chairman & MD Gopal Vittal in call coverage): "We delivered yet another quarter of strong performance, supported by the resilience of our diversified portfolio and sharp execution across businesses." On subscriber quality: "Our postpaid strategy continues to deliver strong outcomes with highest-ever customer additions of 1 million. We added 5 million smartphone customers with an industry-leading ARPU of Rs 264." On the Africa business, commentary framed a transaction/expansion as reflecting "confidence in Africa's long-term growth prospects and the opportunities emerging across connectivity, financial services and enterprise infrastructure."
