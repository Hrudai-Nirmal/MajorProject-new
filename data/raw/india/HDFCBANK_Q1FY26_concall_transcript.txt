--- FINANCIAL RESULTS ---
HDFC Bank reported 8% year-over-year loan growth in Q1 FY26, up from 7% in FY25, signaling early reacceleration. Q1 deposits rose 16% year-on-year while advances grew 8%, lowering the credit-deposit ratio from 110% to 95%. Asset quality remains strong, with retail GNPA (ex-agri) steady at 82 basis points; gross NPA reported at 1.15% and capital adequacy at 19.7% in related full-year coverage.

--- MARGIN / RISK ---
Net interest margin (NIM) declined as expected, reflecting faster repricing of assets (loans) compared to liabilities (deposits) -- roughly 70% of assets are floating-rate while deposits are largely fixed, so recent rate cuts created a margin headwind. Management expects stabilization over coming quarters. Despite the NIM decline, return on assets (ROA) remained stable around 1.9% due to cost efficiencies and lower credit costs -- a mitigating factor explicitly offsetting the margin pressure.

--- CAPITAL ACTIONS ---
The bank received gains from the HDB Financial Services stake sale totaling INR 9,130 crore, which it prudently used to create INR 9,000 crore in floating provisions and INR 1,700 crore in contingent provisions -- floating provisions serve as a counter-cyclical buffer not tied to any specific asset class, an explicit risk-management/conservatism signal. Also declared an interim dividend of INR 5 per share plus the bank's first-ever 1:1 bonus share issuance.
