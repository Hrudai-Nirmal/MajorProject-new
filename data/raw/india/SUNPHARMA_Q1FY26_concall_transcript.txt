--- FINANCIAL RESULTS ---
Sun Pharma reported Q1 FY26 consolidated revenue of INR 137,861 million, up 10.1% year-over-year, with EBITDA margin expanding to 31.1% (up 230 basis points year-over-year). Net profit fell 20% year-over-year despite the revenue rise -- an explicit divergence between top-line growth and bottom-line performance, driven by a higher tax rate and exceptional items.

--- US BUSINESS (explicit risk/mixed signal) ---
US formulation sales were up only 1.5% year-over-year at $473 million, contributing 29% of consolidated revenue -- a muted performance in Sun Pharma's largest single market. Growth in the specialty/innovative portfolio (Ilumya, Cequa, Winlevi, Odomzo) is being offset by decline in the US generics business due to additional competition -- management's own framing of a structural mix shift already underway.

--- PRODUCT PIPELINE ---
Sun Pharma launched Leqselvi in the US for severe alopecia areata, a milestone for the innovative-medicines portfolio; a related patent litigation with Incyte was settled, removing an overhang ahead of launch. Management guided to an approximately $100 million cost impact in FY26, primarily linked to Leqselvi launch investments -- an explicit near-term margin headwind tied to a longer-term growth bet.
