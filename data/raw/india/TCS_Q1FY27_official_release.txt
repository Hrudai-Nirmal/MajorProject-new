TCS reported revenue of US$7,624 million for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Revenue was flat from the previous quarter and increased 2.7% from a year earlier in US dollar terms. Constant-currency sequential growth was 0.4%. Operating margin, excluding an exceptional item, was 24.0%, while net margin was 19.2%. Net income was US$1,460 million on the same basis. Net cash from operations was US$1,310 million, representing 93% of net income.

Management highlighted a US$9.5 billion total contract value for new orders, including a large AI transformation agreement with SKF. Annualized AI revenue reached US$2.6 billion, up 13.6% sequentially. The company also reported partnerships with ServiceNow and other major clients. Management expressed confidence in demand for AI, modernization, cybersecurity and cloud services, while acknowledging geopolitical and macroeconomic headwinds.

Segment performance was mixed. Banking and financial services grew sequentially, while consumer business, life sciences, manufacturing and energy-related segments declined in constant currency.
