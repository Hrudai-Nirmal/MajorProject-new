EAST BRUNSWICK, N.J. | BENGALURU, India – October 15, 2026 : Wipro Limited (NYSE: WIT, BSE: 507685, NSE: WIPRO), a leading AI-powered technology services and consulting company, announced financial results under International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Highlights of the Results

Results for the Quarter ended September 30, 2026:

Gross revenue at ₹247.5 billion ($2,583.0 million 1 ), an increase of 1.1% QoQ and 9.1% YoY.

IT services segment revenue was at $2611.5 million, decrease of 0.1% QoQ and increase of 0.3% YoY.

Non-GAAP constant currency 2 IT Services segment revenue was flat sequentially and increase of 0.6% YoY.

Total Bookings 3 was at $2,595 million, down by 22.9% QoQ in constant currency 2 . Large deal bookings 4 was at $674 million, decrease of 58.6% QoQ in constant currency 2 .

IT services operating margin 5 for Q2’27 was at 16.5%, up by 0.5% QoQ and down by 0.2% YoY.

Net income for the quarter was at ₹32.0 billion ($333.9 million 1 ), decrease of 4.7% QoQ and 1.9% YoY.

Earnings per share for the quarter at ₹3.23 ($0.03 1 ), increase of 0.9% QoQ and increase of 4.2% YoY.

Operating cash flows of ₹53.0 billion ($553.6 million 1 ), increase of 61.3% QoQ and at 165.8% of net income for the quarter.

Voluntary attrition was at 13.8% on a trailing 12-month basis.

Outlook for the Quarter ending December 31, 2026

We expect revenue from our IT Services business segment to be in the range of $2,571 million to $2,625 million*. This translates to sequential guidance of (-)1.5% to (+)0.5% in constant currency terms.

*Outlook for the Quarter ending December 31, 2026, is based on the following exchange rates: GBP/USD at 1.35, Euro/USD at 1.15 , AUD/USD at 0.71, USD/INR at 95.76 and CAD/USD at 0.72

Performance for the Quarter ended September 30, 2026
