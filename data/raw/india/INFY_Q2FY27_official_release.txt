Infosys reported US$5,145 million in revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Constant-currency revenue increased 1.5% year over year and 1.2% sequentially. Operating margin was stable at 21.0%. Free cash flow was US$928 million, a 15.7% year-over-year decrease. Large-deal total contract value reached US$5.2 billion, with 62% net new, and included an agreement worth more than US$2 billion.

Management reported that AI revenue accounted for 10.5% of the total and grew 29% from the preceding quarter. The company cited demand for large transformation programs and continued investment in AI capabilities. Its fiscal 2027 revenue-growth guidance was revised to 1%–2% in constant currency, while operating-margin guidance stayed at 20%–22%.
