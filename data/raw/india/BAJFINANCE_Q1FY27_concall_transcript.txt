--- FINANCIAL RESULTS ---
For the quarter ended 30 June 2026, Bajaj Finance reported consolidated net profit (PAT) of approximately Rs 5,986-6,081 crore, up 27-28% YoY (from Rs 4,699 crore a year earlier per one source). Net interest income (NII) rose 23% YoY to Rs 12,571 crore from Rs 10,228 crore. Profit before tax rose 28% YoY to Rs 8,149 crore. Assets under management (AUM) grew 23.9-24% YoY to Rs 5,46,944 crore, with AUM addition of Rs 36,969 crore in the quarter alone. The company booked 16.13 million new loans and added 5.10 million new customers, taking its total customer franchise to 124.43 million. Return on equity (ROE) was reported above 20%.

--- GUIDANCE / RISK SIGNAL ---
MD Rajeev Jain took a deliberately cautious stance on raising forward guidance despite the strong quarter, saying "one swallow doesn't make a summer" and indicating the company would wait one more quarter of continued momentum before revising guidance on AUM growth, PBT growth, ROA, or ROE. This measured tone from a management team known for aggressive growth targets is itself a notable signal worth flagging in risk analysis (deliberate under-promising vs. genuine caution).

--- MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY ---
Rajeev Jain on AI-driven efficiency: "seeing 20% plus efficiency using AI" in underwriting processes, and "We're doubling down on Fin AI transformation." On the digital platform's scale: "This year the digital platform will deliver Rs 50,000 crores of business," adding "I am targeting that next year we should be delivering Rs 100,000 crores volume between customer centricity, AI and tech transformation and digital transformation." Separate coverage noted AI bots now handle 71% of DIY customer service interactions, reinforcing the AI-transformation narrative as a recurring management theme.
