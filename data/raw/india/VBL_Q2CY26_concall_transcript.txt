--- FINANCIAL RESULTS ---
Varun Beverages reported Q2 CY2026 net revenue of Rs. 84,512.3 million, up 20.4% year over year. EBITDA increased 17.2% to Rs. 23,430.4 million, while PAT increased 15.1% to Rs. 15,253.6 million. Consolidated sales volume grew 19.8% to 466.7 million cases, driven by 14.4% India volume growth and 38.4% international volume growth. EBITDA margin was about 27.7%, compared with 28.5% in the prior-year quarter, so growth was strong but margins moderated. H1 CY2026 net revenue was Rs. 150,254.2 million, up 19.4%, and H1 PAT was Rs. 24,040.7 million, up 16.9%.

--- MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY ---
Management characterized the quarter as strong across markets, citing improved realizations, healthy seasonal demand in India, expanded manufacturing capacity, distribution reach, and continued investment in chilling infrastructure. The company extended its exclusive PepsiCo bottling and trademark license agreement in India to April 2049 and removed a previous restriction requiring VBL to operate only as a special-purpose vehicle for PepsiCo business. Management also highlighted a strategic alliance with Asahi to introduce CALPIS in India and an agreement to acquire a Kenya business that adds go-to-market infrastructure for carbonated soft drinks and energy drinks.

--- RISKS AND ANALYSIS NOTE ---
The sentiment is positive on volume growth, revenue growth, and long-term franchise visibility. The risk signals are margin moderation, higher depreciation from new plants, higher finance costs from the Twizza acquisition, and execution risk from international expansion. Because the company operates in seasonal beverages, weather and summer demand remain important drivers. This document is useful as an India consumer-staples counterpart to Coca-Cola because both disclosures discuss strong brand systems, volume growth, and input-cost or margin tradeoffs.
