Infosys reported US$5,082 million of revenue for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. Constant-currency revenue grew 2.4% year over year and 1.0% sequentially. Operating margin was 21.1%, and free cash flow was about US$955 million. Large-deal total contract value was US$3.6 billion, of which 61% was net new. Management said AI-related revenue accounted for 8.2% of total revenue.

The company revised its fiscal 2027 revenue-growth guidance to 1.5%–3.0% in constant currency and retained operating-margin guidance of 20%–22%. Management described client interest in AI and transformation programs as a source of opportunity. The finance team emphasized disciplined operations and cash generation while referring to a challenging business environment. The combination of deal wins and revised guidance makes this a mixed-sentiment disclosure.
