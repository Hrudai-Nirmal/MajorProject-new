--- FINANCIAL RESULTS ---
Reliance Industries reported a 78% year-on-year jump in consolidated net profit for Q1 FY26, reaching INR 26,994 crore -- the company's best-ever first-quarter profit. Revenue from operations was INR 2,48,660 crore, up 5.3% year-over-year. EBITDA rose to INR 58,024 crore, a 36% increase year-over-year.

--- SEGMENT DETAIL ---
Oil-to-Chemicals (O2C): revenue INR 17,000 crore (note: some coverage reports this figure inconsistently against the larger consolidated revenue base -- flagged for the raw-data cleaning step), up 30%, driven by domestic demand and improved fuel margins.
Jio Platforms: revenue INR 39,173 crore, up 11.8%; Jio crossed 200 million 5G users and 20 million home broadband connections; EBITDA up 24% year-over-year to INR 18,135 crore, with a 210-basis-point increase in operating margin -- a strong, unambiguous growth signal in the telecom segment.
Reliance Retail: revenue INR 90,000 crore, up 12% adjusted for the RCPL demerger -- the demerger adjustment itself is a structural item worth flagging for topic-tagging (corporate restructuring).
