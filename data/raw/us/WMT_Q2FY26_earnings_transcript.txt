--- FINANCIAL RESULTS ---
Walmart's Q2 FY26 revenue rose 5.6% in constant currency, exceeding expectations across all segments. U.S. comparable sales grew 4.6%, e-commerce sales were up 25% year-over-year (some coverage cites 26%), driven by continued strong demand for convenient and fast delivery, with order volumes increasing across segments.

--- MARGIN DETAIL ---
Q2 consolidated gross margin increased 4 basis points on a reported basis, up 9 basis points on an adjusted basis excluding an $80 million one-time restructuring charge at Sam's Club, with improved business mix across segments. In Walmart U.S., margins reflected benefits of strong inventory management, though merchandise category mix remained a headwind as sales growth in grocery and health/wellness outpaced general merchandise -- a mix-shift risk factor explicitly noted by management.

--- GUIDANCE / RISK ---
Despite a $450 million claims-expense headwind (higher than originally anticipated), adjusted operating income rose 0.5%, and annual guidance for adjusted operating income growth was maintained at 3.5%-5.5% on a constant-currency basis. Management indicated they prefer to provide guidance reflecting underlying business expectations, excluding any recovery of tariffs paid -- an explicit statement about how tariff cost pass-through is (not) being baked into guidance.
