--- CEO PREPARED REMARKS (Satya Nadella) ---
It was a very strong close to what was a record fiscal year for us. All up, Microsoft Cloud surpassed $168 billion in annual revenue, up 23%. The rate of innovation and the speed of diffusion is unlike anything we have seen. Azure surpassed $75 billion in annual revenue, up 34%, driven by growth across all workloads. We continue to lead the AI infrastructure wave and took share every quarter this year. We opened new data centers across six continents and now have over 400 data centers across 70 regions, more than any other cloud provider. We stood up more than two gigawatts of new capacity over the past twelve months alone. Every Azure region is now AI-first.

We saw accelerating growth from migrations again this quarter. Nestle, for example, migrated more than 200 SAP instances, 10,000+ servers, and 1.2 petabytes of data to Azure with near-zero business disruption -- one of the largest and most successful migrations in business history.

Microsoft Fabric revenue up 55% year over year, over 25,000 customers, the fastest-growing database product in our history. We launched Azure AI Foundry this year; the Foundry agent service is now used by 14,000 customers. 80% of the Fortune 500 already use Foundry; tokens served by Foundry API grew over seven times this year.

Our family of Copilot apps has surpassed 100 million monthly active users. Microsoft 365 Copilot saw its largest quarter of seat adds since launch, with a record number of customers returning to buy more seats. Barclays will roll out Copilot to 100,000 employees globally; UBS is expanding to all employees; Adobe, KPMG, Pfizer, and Wells Fargo each purchased over 25,000 seats this quarter. Customers created 3 million agents using SharePoint and Copilot Studio this year.

GitHub Copilot: 20 million users, GitHub Copilot Enterprise customers increased 75% quarter over quarter, 90% of the Fortune 100 now use GitHub Copilot. In healthcare, Dragon Copilot documented over 13 million physician-patient encounters this quarter, up nearly seven times year over year.

In closing, we are going through a generational tech shift with AI, and I have never been more confident in my opportunity to drive long-term growth and define what the future looks like.

--- CFO PREPARED REMARKS (Amy Hood) ---
This year, we delivered over $281 billion in revenue, up 15% year over year. Operating income was over $128 billion, up 17% year over year as we invested against the expansive opportunity ahead. This quarter, revenue was $76.4 billion, up 18% in constant currency. Gross margin dollars increased 15% in constant currency, operating income increased 22% in constant currency, EPS $3.65, an increase of 22% in constant currency.

For the first time, commercial bookings were over $100 billion, increasing 30% in constant currency. Commercial remaining performance obligation increased to $368 billion, up 35% in constant currency. Microsoft Cloud revenue was $46.7 billion, ahead of expectations, grew 25% in constant currency. Microsoft Cloud gross margin percentage was 68%, down two points year over year from the impact of scaling AI infrastructure, partially offset by continued efficiency gains. Company gross margin percentage was 69%, down one point year over year, driven by sales mix shift to Azure.

Capital expenditures were $24.2 billion including $5 billion of finance leases. More than half our spend was on long-lived assets that will support monetization over the next fifteen years and beyond. Cash flow from operations was $42.6 billion, up 15%. Free cash flow was $25.6 billion. Effective tax rate approximately 17%. We returned $9.4 billion to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases, bringing total cash return to shareholders to over $37 billion for the full fiscal year.

Outlook: building on strong momentum, we expect to deliver another year of double-digit revenue and operating income growth in FY26. Capital expenditure growth will moderate compared to FY25 with a greater mix of short-lived assets. We expect operating margins to be relatively unchanged year over year. Expect FY26 effective tax rate to be between 19-20%. For Q1: we expect Q1 capital expenditures to be over $30 billion driven by continued strong demand signals. Microsoft Cloud gross margin percentage should be roughly 67%, down year over year driven by the impact of continuing to scale AI infrastructure. In Azure, we expect Q1 revenue growth of approximately 37% in constant currency; we currently expect to remain capacity constrained through the first half of the fiscal year.
