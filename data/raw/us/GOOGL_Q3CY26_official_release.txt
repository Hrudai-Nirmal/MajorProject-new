Alphabet Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results MOUNTAIN VIEW, Calif. – October 28, 2026 – Alphabet Inc. (NASDAQ: GOOG, GOOGL) today announced  financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. • Consolidated Alphabet revenues increased 25% to $127.6 billion, reflecting strong performance across the  business and our 13th consecutive quarter of double-digit revenue growth.  • Google Services revenues increased 11% to $96.3 billion, led by 12% growth in Google Search & other with  the year-over-year Search query growth rate the highest it has been in the last five years. • Google Cloud delivered record growth as revenues increased 103% to $30.7 billion, led by strong growth in  Google Cloud Platform (GCP) across enterprise AI infrastructure and enterprise AI solutions, as well as core  GCP services. TPU systems sales were also a meaningful contributor to revenue growth. • Consolidated Alphabet operating income increased 30% and operating margin was 31.8%. • Net income available to common stockholders was $26.2 billion and EPS was $2.12. Sundar Pichai, CEO of Google and Alphabet, said: “We had a strong quarter and are now on a half-trillion dollar revenue run rate. Our deep full stack approach is  driving momentum across the business, with Alphabet revenues growing 25% year-over year.  Our AI investments are driving overall Search query growth, including commercial queries. In fact, our year-over- year query growth rate is the highest it’s been in the last five years.  Google Cloud revenues grew 103%, powered by huge demand for our AI infrastructure and AI solutions. We’re also  seeing strong demand for our TPU systems across a wide range of customers.  Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon are now leading at the frontier. Overall, our first-party model APIs are now processing  nearly 32 billion tokens per minute, more than four times what we processed a year ago.  On top of that, the Gemini app has reached one billion users—making it the fastest-growing product in Google’s  history—and we surpassed 150 million YouTube Music and Premium subscribers globally, including trials.   We’re excited by the range of big opportunities ahead of us.” Q3 2026 Financial Highlights (unaudited) The following table summarizes our consolidated financial results for the quarters ended September 30, 2025 and  2026 (in millions, except for per share information and percentages).
